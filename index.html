What is FEEDOM?

A tool that puts you in control of your social media.

FEEDOM is a free tool for your browser. Install it once and it works quietly every time you open YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or X.

  • Filters your feed

    Block the topics you don't want, or show only the ones you do. Clickbait, rage bait and politics disappear, and the feed closes up around the gap.

  • Controls your time

    Close the sites in the mornings, at work or at bedtime with weekly blocks, so social media stays in its place.

  • Stops the scroll

    When you keep scrolling, Scroll stop nudges you and gives you a break before the feed swallows another hour.

  • Hides what hijacks you

    Reels and Shorts, trending sidebars, sponsored ads and other attention traps are removed from the page.
