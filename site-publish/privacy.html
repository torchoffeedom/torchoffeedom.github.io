FEEDOM

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026. This is a draft written for the first release and will be reviewed before the extension is published in the stores.

Who is responsible

FEEDOM, based in Slovakia, makes and runs this website and the FEEDOM extension (the "controller" under the GDPR). Contact: torchoffeedom@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The check of posts runs on your device. Titles, channel names, pictures and your activity are never sent to us.
  • FEEDOM needs an account to switch on. The account stores your email address, a list of your devices and your settings. Nothing else.
  • We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.
  • You can delete your account, and everything stored with it, from inside FEEDOM at any time.

The waitlist

If you join the FEEDOM waitlist on this website, we store your email address, the date, which link you came from (for example a post or the site itself), and, if you answer them, your answers to the five survey questions (your situation, the result you want, what frustrates you, what you have tried, and what you would pay). Nothing else: no name, no IP address, no device details.

  • Why: to tell you when FEEDOM launches, to send your beta invite, and to learn what people need so we can build it.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given when you press "Join the Founding Waitlist". You can withdraw it at any time.
  • How long: until we have sent the launch and beta emails and you no longer want them, and in any case you can ask us to delete your entry at any time. Waitlist entries that are never used are deleted within 12 months of the public launch.
  • Where: in the same Supabase project as the accounts (Frankfurt, Germany). The list can be read only by us. We do not sell it or share it.
  • Unsubscribe or delete: write to torchoffeedom@gmail.com and your entry is removed.

The waitlist page sends your email and answers straight from your browser to our Supabase project. It uses no cookies and no analytics.

What stays on your device only

  • The titles and channel names FEEDOM reads from the pages of YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X, to decide what to hide.
  • The pictures it checks. FEEDOM downloads a post's thumbnail from the site's own image servers (the same request the page makes) and checks it with a model that is built into the extension. The picture is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your activity log: which posts were hidden and why, kept for 7 or 30 days as you choose, and deletable in Settings.
  • Your own words and account lists, what you taught FEEDOM, and the Camera shield state.
  • The lock password, if you switch the lock on. Signed in with Google, it exists only on your device. Signed in with email, it is your account password, checked against the account.

With Google sign-in, Google tells our authentication provider your email address. We use only that.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the database and the sign-in. Our project is in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • An email provider delivers the codes you ask for. It sees your email address and the message. It will be named here once chosen.
  • Google only if you choose "Continue with Google".

These providers act on our behalf and may not use the data for their own purposes. The extension talks to them from its background only; websites cannot reach your account.

Why we may use it (legal basis)

To provide FEEDOM to you under our agreement with you (the account, sync and device list), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. When you delete your account in FEEDOM (Settings, Account, Delete my account), your account and the settings and devices stored with it are deleted at once. Short-lived backups at our hosting provider may keep a copy for a limited time.

Your rights

You can download a copy of everything stored on our server for you from inside FEEDOM (Settings, Account, Download my data). You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to its use, and you can complain to a data protection authority. In Slovakia that is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky). Write to torchoffeedom@gmail.com. Deleting your account in FEEDOM is the quickest way to delete your data.

Children

An account must be created by an adult, or by a parent for a child. People under 16 should not create an account on their own. FEEDOM can be used on a child's browser by a parent who is signed in.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits as described in its own privacy statement. The extension adds no tracking.

Limited use of Google user data

If you sign in with Google, FEEDOM receives only your email address. It is used to run your account, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared except with the providers listed above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new date. Larger changes will also be shown in FEEDOM.
